LIMS (Lost Item Management System) Support Knowledge Base

Overview:
- LIMS helps users report lost and found items, discover potential matches, and coordinate safe item recovery.
- The platform includes user dashboards, notifications, claims workflows, reporting tools, wallet/coins, ratings, badges, and admin moderation.

Reporting Lost or Found Items:
- Users sign in and submit item details such as title, category, description, location, date, and optional image.
- Item type can be "lost" or "found".
- Accurate details improve matching quality.

Matching:
- The system computes potential matches between lost and found items.
- Users can review match suggestions in their dashboard.
- Match confidence is displayed in the interface.

Claims and Contact Sharing:
- A user can request contact for a proposed match.
- Contact sharing follows platform workflow and is not public by default.
- Claim status can move through stages such as pending, contact_shared, in_progress, completed, or rejected.
- A claim is considered completed after both sides confirm handoff steps.

Notifications:
- Users receive notifications for matches, contact requests, claim updates, badge events, and related workflow actions.
- Notifications can be marked as read.

Coins and Wallet:
- New users receive starter coins.
- Coins are used for certain actions such as reporting lost items.
- Successful return outcomes can reward coins.
- Users can request coin recharge through payment methods; admins review these requests.
- Wallet shows coin balance and recent transaction/request history.

Ratings and Badges:
- Users can rate each other after claim completion.
- Ratings contribute to average score and trust context.
- Badges represent trust signals, including return-related badges and warning badges when reports accumulate.

Suspicious User Handling:
- Users can report suspicious behavior through claim/report workflows.
- Warning-related trust signals and admin review tools exist for platform safety.

Admin Moderation:
- Admins review pending/reported items and can verify, reject, or remove content per platform rules.
- Admins can review claims, suspicious users, payment requests, and logs.
- Admin actions and safety workflows are part of moderation controls.

Account Management:
- Users can sign up, log in, update profile details, and manage account actions through the dashboard/profile pages.
- Password reset is available through the forgot/reset password flow.

Privacy and Terms:
- LIMS has a combined Privacy Policy & Terms page.
- The platform collects account, item, workflow, and operational data for service and safety purposes.
- Users must follow truthful reporting, anti-fraud, anti-spam, and respectful communication expectations.

Guidelines and Safety:
- Meet in public places.
- Verify item details before handoff.
- Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.
- Use caution with unknown individuals.

When to escalate:
- If a user asks for account-specific intervention, payment disputes, unresolved moderation, or uncertain policy outcomes, advise contacting LIMS administrators for direct assistance.
