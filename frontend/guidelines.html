LIMS User Guidelines

Safe, accurate, and trustworthy usage for better recovery outcomes.

Welcome

Welcome to the Lost Item Management System (LIMS). LIMS helps users report lost items, report found items, discover possible matches, connect with potential owners, and safely recover belongings.

How to Report a Lost Item
  1. Sign in to your account.
  2. Open the Report Lost Item page.
  3. Enter accurate item title, category, description, location, date lost, and photos if available.
  4. Submit the report.
  5. The system will search for possible matches automatically.
How to Report a Found Item
  1. Sign in to your account.
  2. Open the Report Found Item page.
  3. Provide detailed information.
  4. Upload photos when possible.
  5. Submit the report.
  6. Potential owners may be notified automatically.
Matching and Claims
  • The system automatically searches for potential matches.
  • Users can review suggested matches.
  • Contact information is only shared through the platform workflow.
  • Do not share sensitive personal information publicly.
Safe Exchange Recommendations
  • Meet in public places.
  • Verify item details before handing over the item.
  • Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.
  • Use caution when interacting with unknown individuals.
Coin System
  • New users receive starter coins.
  • Coins are used for lost item reports.
  • Successfully returning found items can earn rewards.
  • Coins can be recharged through approved payment methods.
Community Rules
  • Provide truthful information.
  • Upload accurate item descriptions.
  • Respect other users.
  • Avoid spam reports and fraudulent claims.

Violation may result in warnings, restrictions, or account suspension.

Reporting Abuse

Users may report suspicious behavior, fraudulent claims, fake items, or harassment.

Reported activity may be reviewed by administrators.

Final Notes

LIMS is designed to improve the recovery of lost property while maintaining fairness, safety, and trust between users.
