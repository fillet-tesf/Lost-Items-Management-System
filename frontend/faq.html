Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about accounts, reports, claims, coins, and platform safety.

How do I create an account?
Open Sign Up, enter your details, accept the Privacy Policy & Terms, and submit.

Why do I need to verify my information?
Accurate information helps reduce fraud, improves trust, and supports safe contact-sharing workflows.

How do I reset my password?
Use the Forgot Password flow from the login page and follow the instructions.

How do I report a lost item?
Sign in, open Report Item, select lost type, add full details and submit.

Can I edit my report later?
Yes. You can update your own active report from your dashboard item management screens.

What happens after I submit a report?
The report enters platform workflows, and matching logic can suggest potential found-item links.

How do I report a found item?
Sign in, submit a found-item report with accurate category, location, date, and photos when possible.

How does matching work?
The system compares item attributes to surface likely matches and notifies relevant users.

How do I claim an item?
From a suggested match, request contact through the platform process.

What happens after I request contact information?
The finder receives a notification and may share contact details through approved options.

When is an item marked as successfully returned?
After the handoff is confirmed in the platform workflow by both sides.

What are coins used for?
Coins support selected report actions and platform incentives.

How can I earn coins?
Users can earn rewards through successful return outcomes.

How do coin recharges work?
Recharge requests are submitted and processed via approved payment and review flows.

What are user ratings?
Ratings reflect user experience after claim completion and help others assess trust.

What do badges mean?
Badges indicate trust milestones and warning signals based on activity patterns.

What is a warning badge?
A warning badge may appear when multiple reports are received and can be reviewed through platform processes.

Why was my item rejected?
Items may be rejected for incomplete details, policy violations, duplicates, or suspicious content.

How can I appeal a decision?
Use available profile/support mechanisms to submit an appeal for manual review.

Is my contact information public?
No. Contact details are shared only through platform-controlled claim steps.

Who can see my personal information?
Access is limited to system and workflow needs according to platform policy and role-based controls.
